# Portal notifications

Administrators open **Communication → Notifications**, choose **Guardian** or **Staff**, enter a title and message, and select **Share notification**. The message is saved immediately to every active portal account in that group. Staff accounts must also have an active, unarchived staff profile. Accounts created later do not receive old announcements.

Guardians and staff open **Notifications** to read messages addressed to their account and mark them as read. The Refresh button checks for new messages. Only administrators can compose notifications or view sent history. Other recipients and their read states are private.

Notifications use the portal exclusively. No emails, SMS, WhatsApp messages, or browser push messages are created. The notification email worker has been removed, so previously queued notification emails are no longer processed by this version. Existing portal messages remain readable. Password-reset emails continue using the existing email service independently.
