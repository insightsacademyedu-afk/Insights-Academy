# Notifications

Administrators can open **Communication → Notifications** to send a title and message to selected guardian accounts, the guardian accounts linked to selected students, or all active registered guardian accounts. Selecting overlapping students and guardians delivers one copy per account. Students without an active linked parent login cannot be selected.

Guardians open **Notifications** in their portal navigation to read academy messages and mark them as read. Inbox records are private to the recipient account. The inbox and sent history have a Refresh button. Broadcast recipients are fixed at send time; later registrations do not receive old announcements.

Every notification is saved to portal inboxes immediately. Email is queued for recipients with an email address on their login account. The queue uses the same email configuration as password resets: `RESEND_API_KEY` and `RESEND_FROM`, or `SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_PORT`, `SMTP_SECURE`, `SMTP_USER`, `SMTP_PASS` and optionally `SMTP_FROM`. `CLIENT_URL` supplies the portal link in emails. Email bodies are plain text and recipients receive separate emails.

The send request attempts the first email immediately and reports provider acceptance or failure. Remaining emails stay in the database queue. While the administrator keeps Notifications open, the page submits bounded queue-processing requests every ten seconds; **Process queued emails** also processes a batch immediately. These authenticated requests resume older pending broadcasts even when the background worker is unavailable. Closing the page can pause request-driven delivery on hosting that sleeps; reopen Notifications to resume it.

Run the API through `server/server.js` (`npm run dev` or `npm start`) to also process saved email jobs in the background. The worker checks every five seconds and resumes pending deliveries after a restart. Sent history reports email acceptance by the provider, pending emails, failures and accounts without an email address. Provider acceptance does not verify inbox arrival. Failed emails are not retried automatically; portal messages remain available. An interrupted email whose delivery is uncertain is marked failed after five minutes to avoid automatic duplicates.

For new delivery failures, admin history also shows a safe explanation of provider restrictions, domain verification errors, authentication errors or rate limits. Guardian recipient search shows the registered destination email; accounts without one receive portal messages only. The Resend test sender (`onboarding@resend.dev`) can send only to the Resend account owner's address. To email other guardians, verify an academy domain in Resend and set `RESEND_FROM` to an address on that domain in the deployed server environment. If the provider accepted the email but it did not arrive, inspect its delivery/bounce logs and the recipient's spam folder. Existing failures from older versions do not contain a recorded reason.

If email is not configured when sending, portal delivery still succeeds and the send result and history show that email was unavailable. Configure the service before sending subsequent messages. No live email is sent by the automated tests.
